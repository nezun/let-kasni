# Email signature assets

Versioned public assets used by the LetKasni email signature.

- `v1/marko-jovanovic-portrait.png`: circular crop supplied for Marko Jovanović.
- `v1/letkasni-logo.png`: LetKasni logo rendered at 496 × 144 px.
- `v1/instagram.png`: Instagram glyph from Simple Icons, packaged in LetKasni blue.
- `v1/facebook.png`: Facebook glyph from Simple Icons, packaged in LetKasni blue.

Instagram and Facebook source glyphs:

- <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/instagram.svg>
- <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/facebook.svg>
